# Question 1

Mary predeceased Frank. Couple has one joint daughter. Frank also has two children from a prior relationship. Mary’s intestate estate = $800,000.

## Identify controlling UPC rule

- Spousal share is governed by UPC § 2-102.[[1]](DE&T%202%2026a4fdbee4508062ae0dc9440953bb0e.md)
- Scenario fits § 2-102(3): “all of the decedent’s surviving descendants are also descendants of the surviving spouse AND the surviving spouse has one or more surviving descendants who are not descendants of the decedent.”[[2]](DE&T%202%2026a4fdbee4508062ae0dc9440953bb0e.md)[[3]](https://www.notion.so/UPC-Intestacy-Master-2694fdbee45080c69228df7f6fde8646?pvs=21)

## Apply § 2-102(3)

- Spouse takes: $225,000 + 1/2 of the balance of the intestate estate.
- Compute balance: $800,000 − $225,000 = $575,000.
- Half the balance to spouse: 1/2 × $575,000 = $287,500.
- Spouse total = $225,000 + $287,500 = $512,500 to Frank.

## Remainder to descendants

- Remainder = $800,000 − $512,500 = $287,500.
- Heirs other than spouse take by § 2-103(a)(1) “to the decedent’s descendants by representation,” using UPC § 2-106(b) (per capita at each generation).[[4]](DE&T%202%2026a4fdbee4508062ae0dc9440953bb0e.md)
- Only one surviving descendant (the joint daughter). Thus, she receives the entire remainder $287,500.

## Answer (Q1)

- Frank (surviving spouse): $512,500
- Daughter (Mary + Frank’s child): $287,500

# Question 2

Frank predeceased Mary. Same family facts. Frank’s intestate estate = $600,000.

## Identify controlling UPC rule

- Scenario fits § 2-102(4): “one or more of the decedent’s surviving descendants are not descendants of the surviving spouse.” Frank’s two prior children are not Mary’s descendants.[[5]](DE&T%202%2026a4fdbee4508062ae0dc9440953bb0e.md)

## Apply § 2-102(4)

- Spouse takes: $150,000 + 1/2 of the balance.
- Compute balance: $600,000 − $150,000 = $450,000.
- Half the balance to spouse: 1/2 × $450,000 = $225,000.
- Spouse total = $150,000 + $225,000 = $375,000 to Mary.

## Remainder to descendants

- Remainder = $600,000 − $375,000 = $225,000.
- Frank’s descendants by representation under § 2-103(a)(1) and § 2-106(b). Three surviving children (two from prior relationship + one joint daughter), none predeceased.
- Equal per capita at the nearest generation (children): $225,000 ÷ 3 = $75,000 each.[[6]](https://www.notion.so/UPC-Intestacy-Master-2694fdbee45080c69228df7f6fde8646?pvs=21)

## Answer (Q2)

- Mary (surviving spouse): $375,000
- Each of Frank’s three children: $75,000

# Question 3(b) - Pennsylvania Law

## Family Tree Analysis

From the provided family tree:

- **A** (decedent) - died intestate
- **Children**: B, C, D (all predeceased A)
- **Grandchildren**:
    - **Living**: g, h, j

    - **Predeceased without issue**: I
- **Great-grandchildren**: m, n, o, p, q, r, s, t (all survive)

## Pennsylvania Intestacy Rule (PA § 2104(1))

The controlling Pennsylvania statute is **PA § 2104(1) - Taking in different degrees**:

> "The part of the estate passing to any such persons shall be divided into as many equal shares as there shall be persons in the nearest degree of consanguinity to the decedent living and taking shares therein and persons in that degree who have died before the decedent and have left issue to survive him who take shares therein."
> 

## Step-by-Step Application

### Step 1: Identify the Nearest Degree with Living Heirs

- **Children's generation**: B, C, D all predeceased → no living heirs at this level
- **Grandchildren's generation**: g, h, j are living → this is the nearest degree with survivors

### Step 2: Count Total Shares at Grandchildren Level

- **Living grandchildren taking shares**: g, h, j (3 persons)
- **Deceased grandchildren with surviving issue**: F (has m, n), K (has r, s), L (has t) (3 persons)
- **Deceased grandchildren without issue**: I (creates no share)
- **Total shares**: 3 + 3 = **6 equal shares**

### Step 3: Distribute Shares

**Living Grandchildren** (each gets 1/6):

- **g**: 1/6
- **h**: 1/6
- **j**: 1/6

**Deceased Grandchildren's Shares Pass by Representation**:

- **F's share (1/6)** passes to F's children m and n equally:
    - **m**: 1/6 ÷ 2 = **1/12**
    - **n**: 1/6 ÷ 2 = **1/12**
- **K's share (1/6)** passes to K's children r and s equally:
    - **r**: 1/6 ÷ 2 = **1/12**
    - **s**: 1/6 ÷ 2 = **1/12**
- **L's share (1/6)** passes to L's child t:
    - **t**: **1/6**

**Great-grandchildren of Living Grandchildren** (get nothing because their parents are alive):

- **o** (child of g): **0**
- **p** (child of h): **0**
- **q** (child of h): **0**
